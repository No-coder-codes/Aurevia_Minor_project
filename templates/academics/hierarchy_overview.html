Aurevia Dual-Branch Governance Framework
Executive separation of academic curricula from operational administration
Provost Oversight

Academic Schools, Departments, and Degree Programmes maintain curriculum integrity, faculty appointments, research grants, and student viva assessments under the Provost.

Registrar Operations

Admissions Office, Residential Hostels, Library Services, IT Infrastructure, and Finance operate under the Registrar to deliver seamless campus facilities.

Dual-Reporting Admissions

Candidate applications bridge both branches: administrative verification is conducted by the Registrar, while academic suitability is reviewed by School faculty.
